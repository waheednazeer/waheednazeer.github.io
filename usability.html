User Usability Test

Key Components of Usability

I use the tests listed below to assess user usability.

  • I asked my three coworkers to look through every page in my portfolio to assess its navigation.
  • Test it on laptops and smartphones.
  • Tests all links on all pages.
  • For effortless reading, I performed an accessibility test for the font's color and size.
  • Checked errors users make, their severity, and how easily they can recover.
  • To find out how pleasant and engaging the experience is, test the portfolio performance.
  • Check the efficiency at which users can find the required information on the portfolio.

Findings and Enhancements:

  • Found and fixed a broken connection.
  • Improved navigation by adding a fixed navigation bar.
  • Improved readability by adjusting font size and color contrast.
  • Enhanced mobile responsiveness for better smartphone viewing.
