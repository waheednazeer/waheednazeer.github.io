Skills

Network Design, Project Management, Vendor Coordination, Technical Implementation, Testing & Commissioning, Documentation.

Challenges

  • Minimize downtime during the transition.
  • Meet the organization's specific requirements for scalability, reliability, and security.
Ethical Integration
  • Throughout the project, I ensured adherence to ethical standards by promoting transparency with stakeholders.
  • I ensured data privacy and security by implementing robust security measures
  • I fostered a collaborative environment among team members and vendors, encouraging open communication and mutual respect.
Refinements

Impact
  • The NGN-ready solution significantly enhanced the organization's network capabilities,
  • Enabling it to support advanced services such as VoIP, video conferencing, and high-speed data transfer.
  • The project improved overall network performance, scalability, and reliability, positioning the organization for future growth and technological advancements.
